Dexter

Dexter follows the life of a Miami Metro Blood Spatter Analyst who is secretely a serial killer. He battles his own police co-workers and sister to keep his crimes a secret, trying to contain it from the inside. He follows a code instilled by his father, a former police officer, who knew from a young age that he was different then the other kids.

Dexter also has a girlfriend, Rita, and her two children from a previous marriage. He does all he can to protect them and his family.

  1. Michael C. Hall as Dexter Morgan
  2. Jennifer Carpenter as Debra Morgan
  3. David Zayas as Angel Bautista
  4. Julie Benz as Rita
